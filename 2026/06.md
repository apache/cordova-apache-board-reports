A platform for building native mobile applications using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

## Community Health

Overall, the community health is strong. The ASF Project Statistics gives the project a Community Health Score (Chi): 4.70 (Healthy)

This quarter, we primarily focused on improving our iOS platform, plugins, and release process.

We continue to see valuable contributions from a dedicated group of individuals who actively test, report issues, and submit PRs to address their findings in future releases. A large portion of this quarter's activity has focused on improvements to the In-App Browser plugin, as well as the iOS and Android platforms. There have also been a small number of contributions to other plugins, tooling, and platforms.

Overall, the project remains stable and continues to see strong activity.

Github discussions is how our community supports each other and is live at https://github.com/apache/cordova/discussions. All discussions are forwarded to the 'issues' list.
